Frequently asked

Questions, answered

info@rootednutrition.co
Is there a free first call?

Yes. A complimentary 20 minute call to talk about your goals and see if it is the right fit.

Do you offer virtual sessions?

Yes. Choose virtual or in person in Calgary when you book.

What happens in the initial assessment?

A full look at your health history, nutrition, lifestyle and goals. It includes The Rooted Kickstart, your foundation meal plan.

Will I be put on a strict diet?

No. Recommendations are based on what you need, not the latest diet trend.

How often are coaching sessions?

Weekly or every two weeks, depending on your goal. A three month bundle is available.

Where are you located?

7777 Macleod Trail SW, Unit 140, Calgary, Alberta. Virtual sessions are also available.

Is this covered by insurance?

Coverage for holistic nutrition varies by plan. Please check with your benefits provider.

Do you diagnose or treat medical conditions?

No. Rooted Nutrition provides nutrition and lifestyle education and support alongside the care you receive from your physician.
